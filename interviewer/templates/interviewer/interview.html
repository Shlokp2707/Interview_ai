Interview Rules & Security Guidelines

Please read the following rules and security warning criteria carefully. These rules are enforced automatically by the AI proctoring engine.

📋 General Requirements

  • You must sit in a quiet, well-lit private space.
  • Do not use headphones, earbuds, or other external communication devices.
  • Keep your camera and microphone enabled throughout the session.
  • Ensure you do not switch browser tabs, minimize the window, or open other applications.

⚠️ AI Proctoring Warning Criteria

The system monitors your camera feed and ambient audio. The following events will trigger immediate warnings:

  • Looking Away: Looking away from the screen for more than 4 seconds.
  • Out of Camera Frame: If your face is not detected for more than 5 seconds.
  • Multiple Faces: If another person's face appears in the frame (strictly no two persons on screen).
  • Photo/Spoofing Check: A real candidate must be present. Holding a static photograph or phone screen to the camera will fail liveness checks.
  • High Noise Activity: High audio levels or external voices while not answering questions will trigger alerts.

🚫 Disqualification & Termination Criteria

  • Each triggered alert increments your warning count.
  • If you accumulate 5 security warnings, the session is terminated instantly and your application status is marked as rejected.
